# Vietnamese

Owner: "viết tự nhiên như blog ấy"; of the old prose, "trò viết không giống tiếng Việt". How a sentence reads and which calques to drop
is [voice.md](voice.md) **(đề xuất)**; write to it and run its checklist (§8) before the passes below.

Owner, 2026-11-26: "giờ viết tiếng việt riêng tiếng anh riêng". Vietnamese copy is written in Vietnamese; a term's English name sits on
its own label, never inside the sentence, and the English locale is written on its own ([voice.md](voice.md) §3).

All learner-facing Vietnamese is checked with four skills: translationese-cleaner-vi, humanizer-vi, style-guide-vi,
grammar-checker-vi. **(đề xuất)** They check, they do not rewrite: where one would flatten the blog voice of voice.md, the voice wins.
Replaces: "four passes, in this order".

The four skills come from <https://github.com/longhang2004/vietnamese-humanizer> (MIT); that repository is the reference for what
they check. Its core rules, which every learner-facing sentence holds:

- Topic–comment order: say what the sentence is about first, then what holds of it; not the English subject–verb mould.
- Plain verbs: "kiểm tra", "gửi", "sửa", never "tiến hành kiểm tra" or "thực hiện việc gửi".
- No stock openings or closers: "Trong bài viết này…", "Hôm nay chúng ta sẽ…", "Hy vọng bài viết hữu ích".
- No hype adjectives ("tuyệt vời", "vượt trội", "cực kỳ mạnh mẽ"); a number or nothing.
- Drop stacked connectives: "đồng thời", "qua đó", "từ đó" piled at the head of sentences.
- One register per voice: a voice keeps one way of addressing the reader and one level of formality from start to end.
- Keep numbers, negations and hedges exactly: a pass never changes "khoảng 2 giây", "không", "có thể".

Terms: "xóa", "bảo đảm", "chống trùng". Thousands take a dot: 499.000đ.

The old exception that "key" and "lock" stay English inside the sentence is withdrawn by the 2026-11-26 rule: page copy says it in
Vietnamese and puts the English name on the separate label; in code they stay identifiers. Spelling stays "khóa" (khóa chính, khóa ngoại).
